Short Story Summary


Once there were 3 little pigs. They all went off on their own for reasons. Each one built a house out of increasingly better structural materials. Soon, a big bad wolf comes by each of the 3 pig's houses, and attempts to destroy their homes in order to devour the swines. The first two pig's homes are blown down, leading them to gather in the third pig's house. This house was made of brick, so it didn't falter to the wolf. The wolf, tired of blowing wind at homes to no success, attempts to enter through the chimney, in which he falls straight down into a pot of boiling water, where he dies. Very dark for a children's story, but yeah.
